# Jehangir — People Behind the Visit

Independent student case study of Jehangir Art Gallery, Mumbai. Visitor personas lead the study; artist perspectives, exhibition economics, field photographs and recommendations provide context.

## Run locally

Requires Node.js 24 or later.

```
npm ci
npm run dev
```

`npm test` checks the exhibition estimator and research references. `npm run build` produces the static website in `dist/`. GitHub Actions publishes the main branch to GitHub Pages.

## Evidence conventions

- Five interpretive visitor personas, not a representative segmentation survey.
- Fourteen recording sections reviewed, with overlaps and continuations acknowledged. No verified independent respondent count or mean rating is claimed.
- Anonymous relevant excerpts only; full transcripts and unrelated personal details are excluded.
- Source 14 is a separate uncertain-venue appendix, excluded from Jehangir persona evidence.
- The photographed fee notice states effective **1 July 2027**. It is not presented as current November 2026 pricing. Amounts are transcribed as photographed.
- The historical five-year-wait report does not establish a guaranteed current wait.
- Original photographs were converted to WebP with metadata removed. Descriptive artwork captions are not verified artwork titles or artist attributions.
- Team names follow the supplied photo matches: Aastha, Omkar, Tanmay, Tanaya, Riya and Ojas. Member reflections are clearly labelled editorial syntheses from shared evidence, not individually recorded field notes.

## Expanded research and interaction

- Conceptual CSS 3D gallery scene linked to the five visitor personas, with user-controlled rotation and a flattened reduced-motion mode.
- Google Maps review retrieval through Firecrawl on 26 November 2026: 60 newest records (11–25 November 2026 UTC) and a separate 30 lowest-rated diagnostic records (November 2016–September 2026 UTC). No pooled average or representative complaint-frequency claim.
- The Maps listing snapshot was 4.5/5 from 12,853 ratings. This is separate from the review sample. Of the newest 60, only 16 have written text. A non-visit record and a general review-style summary are excluded from thematic first-person evidence.
- Public review data publishes anonymous record IDs, dates, ratings, source links and concise research summaries. Reviewer names, images and full review text are omitted.
- Official practice comparisons: National Gallery London, National Gallery Singapore, Art Institute of Chicago, and National Gallery of Victoria. These are four-region benchmarks, not a comprehensive global ranking or a like-for-like institutional comparison.
- Interactive issue explorer, practice comparison and pilot-bundle support map. The support map is a design mapping, not a prediction of measured improvement.
- The team-provided Google share link was checked in a browser and showed the same gallery, 4.5 rating and 12,853 reviews as the venue retrieved through Firecrawl. The short-link scrape itself did not resolve reliably; review collection used the matched Maps venue.

Artwork rights remain with their respective creators. This project is independent of the gallery.
